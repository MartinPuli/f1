# JEV Prix

A single full-screen circuit, calm pastel landscape and Formula-inspired open-wheel cars. Cream floating controls, warm rust primary action, ink typography and five distinct driver colors. Fredoka display, Nunito interface. The scene is the product; setup, results/archive, key settings and rename all live in native modal dialogs. Orbitable chase camera is the default, with driver selection and aerial/circuit views. Reduced-motion preference removes extra camera effects and decorative movement. Mobile controls stack below the driver selector, with a separate replay timeline when needed.
