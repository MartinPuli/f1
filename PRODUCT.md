# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
The user watches five autonomous agents race and compares their results.
## Product Purpose
A Three.js racing experiment in which drivers know they are on a circuit but see only a local 42 m observation, not the complete map.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Five independent drivers; generated circuits; three laps; real Jev integration plus clearly labelled local demonstration; lap timing, off-track events and collisions. The primary screen contains the full-screen race and minimal controls. Results belong in a separate tab.
## Brand Commitments
User explicitly requests a friendly, colourful kart-racing visual world inspired by Mario Kart and an effective camera following behind individual cars. Preserve Spanish interface and the ability to follow any of the five drivers. Use original environment and vehicles.
## Evidence on Hand
The local simulation and TypeSafe proxy are implemented. Live model performance is not verified without a user key. Demo results are not AI benchmark results.
